# Grounded market directions

The visitor is testing a fruit photo in a UAS demo that uses a contemporary fruit-market context. The page remains a familiar web tool; each world contributes only its type, palette, density, and one signature move.

1. **Fruit-stall awning** — painted cloth and bamboo signboard; bands of fruit-color fabric, broad caps, and one striped canopy rhythm. Recognizable as a neighborhood stall; the canopy can become the page's shell color and fruit category tabs.
2. **Crate stencil grades** — reusable wood and plastic fruit crates; deep crate green, stenciled white labels, citrus grade marks, and a stacked density. The category tags are familiar to vendors; one stamped class mark can define the result state.
3. **Shelf-edge price strips** — laminated shelf rails and replaceable plastic tags; color blocks and narrow alignment lines organize produce types. Its repeated strips make supported fruit easy to scan without inventing new navigation.
4. **Market inspection ticket** — compact fibrous price cards and receipt marks; leaf-green, citrus-yellow, and tomato-red labels, a sturdy workhorse typeface, and a single result stamp. The visitor can read each output like one clear market label. The user selected this market-application context.
5. **Fruit-protection stickers** — adhesive labels on waxed paper and mesh sleeves; small fruit marks repeat around one bold status label. The material is close to how fruit is sold, while status color is the signature.
6. **Daily produce circular** — newsprint promotion sheets and printed market notices; one tight column of changing fruit names and a large offer mark. The vocabulary is recognizable but risks reading as a promotion instead of a tool.
7. **Woven market baskets** — rattan, mesh, and produce netting; open spacing and crossing bands create the rhythm. It relates to carrying fruit but is too weak as the information system for a classifier.

## Challenger review

- **Split-flap concourse:** declined; its fixed columns suggest a public status board and do not fit the one-photo workflow. Keep its clean alignment discipline.
- **Pocket timetable slide rack:** declined; its schedule topology has no use for this image task. Keep strong distinct state marks.
- **Character goods catalog:** declined; mascots distract from the photo and can imply quality claims. Keep one-fruit-per-result clarity.
- **Lexicon shoulder-and-plate:** declined; dictionary navigation overstates the amount of information the result carries. Keep its controlled measure and explicit labels.
- **Emigre bitmap specimen:** declined; poster lettering would weaken routine reading and mobile legibility. Keep a decisive type hierarchy.
- **Zoo and gardens guide map:** declined; spatial regions do not represent this single-image request. Keep its discipline of separate color roles.
